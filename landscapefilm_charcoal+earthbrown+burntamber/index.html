01 / FOUR FIELD MONOGRAPHS

Four landscapes recorded on 16mm stock over four seasonal cycles

Each monograph isolates a single geological or architectural site at twilight, documenting light decay, wind load, and human scale without artificial illumination.

Study 01 · Steppe Monoliths

Four upright volcanic markers aligned along an abandoned coastal track. Shot across forty minutes of solar descent with locked exposure to trace how tall meadow grass absorbs remaining horizon warmth.
