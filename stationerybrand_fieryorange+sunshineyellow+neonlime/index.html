02 / DIY IDEAS & STUDIO TEMPLATES

Four practical projects from one drawer

Select any template to inspect its roll widths, application steps, and add the paired rolls straight to your bag.

10 MIN · 24 MM Lime + Fiery Orange

Color-Coded Spine Index

Align horizontal bands at fixed heights across sketchbook and binder spines for instant shelf sorting.

  1. Mark 30 mm height intervals with a pencil tick on each binder spine.
  2. Wrap a 24 mm Neon Lime or Fiery Orange band across spine and front hinge.
  3. Label course code in fine permanent black ink directly over the matte tape.
25 MIN · 15 MM Jet Grid + Sunshine

Grid Wall Planner

Map a clean five-day studio sprint straight onto painted drywall with zero residue when the project wraps.

  1. Run 15 mm Sunshine Yellow horizontal rows to divide morning and afternoon blocks.
  2. Snap vertical column lines using 15 mm Chalk Grid tape for crisp day boundaries.
  3. Pin task slips using short 40 mm Neon Lime strips torn at a 15-degree angle.
5 MIN · 48 MM Fiery Wide + Lime

Parcel Seal Strips

Reinforce kraft mailers and sketchbook shipments with wide 48 mm crepe that bends cleanly over box corners.

  1. Center 48 mm Fiery Orange crepe along the top carton seam with 35 mm overhang.
  2. Cross-lock the seam midpoint with a short 24 mm Neon Lime address tag strip.
  3. Burnish the fold edges by thumb for instant fiber bond on recycled cardboard.
8 MIN · 15 MM Dotted Trio Pack

Notebook Tabs

Fold back-to-back paper tape flags along bullet journal edges so chapter dividers stay crisp all semester.

  1. Tear a 50 mm strip of 15 mm Dotted tape and stick half onto the page edge.
  2. Fold the remaining 25 mm back over the reverse face, leaving a 12 mm tab.
  3. Write chapter numbers on the overhang using a 0.5 mm pigment liner.
