02 / DAILY FLIGHT SCHEDULE

Feeding & emergence hours

Keeper rounds structure each day under the glass. Morning warmth prompts fresh eclosion in the emergence cabinet, while midday fruit trays draw canopy flyers to eye level.

Time Keeper Round & Zone Duration
  • 10:30

    Chrysalis Emergence Release

    Zone A · Emergence Cabinet

    Newly eclosed Morpho and Swallowtail adults dry their wings for two hours before keepers transfer them onto warm hibiscus branches.

    Daily · 25 min
  • 13:00

    Tropical Fruit & Nectar Feeding

    Zone B · Heliconia Walk

    Sliced papaya, mango, and Seville orange halves are placed on copper trays as sun angles peak above the flowering Pentas beds.

    Daily · 40 min
  • 15:30

    Twilight Mist & Atlas Moth View

    Zone D · Cedar Pavilion

    Overhead misting lines lift ambient humidity to 82% as resting Atlas and Comet moths expand their wings before dusk.

    Daily · 30 min
