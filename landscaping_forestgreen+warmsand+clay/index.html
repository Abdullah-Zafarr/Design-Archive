02 / FOUR-SEASON PLANTING PHASES

Structure in winter, canopy in summer

MAR – MAY · Early foliage unfurl & bulb succession

Fresh emerald emergence under dappled canopy

Woodland bulbs push through leaf mould before deciduous canopies close overhead, establishing clean structural lines across damp limestone paths.

Canopy layer
Amelanchier lamarckii, Betula pendula
Perennial matrix
Dryopteris filix-mas, Epimedium, Narcissus Thalia
Groundcover
Asarum europaeum, woodland moss

HARDSCAPE & MASONRY

Stone specification

Sub-base & drainage
150 mm Type 3 permeable aggregate, 1:60 fall away from thresholds
Mulch & soil dressing
60 mm composted bark fines applied post-frost

Light selective cutback of winter stems in early March
