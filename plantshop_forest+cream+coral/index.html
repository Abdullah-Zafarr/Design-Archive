03 BOTANICAL CARE

Keep canopies thriving

Deep soak, full drain

Check the upper 5 cm of bark mix before watering. Pour 1.5 to 2 litres slowly around the root ball until water exits the stoneware base, then empty the saucer after 15 minutes.
