03 / MILAN WORKSHOP & STORES

Ten weeks at the bench

Every VELLUTO sofa enters production only after your velvet batch is reserved. Follow each stage from beech frame joinery to final Milan dispatch.

  1. WEEKS 01–02

    Kiln-Dried Beech Frame

    Mortise-and-tenon joinery milled to 108 cm seat depth and tensioned with Italian elastic webbing.

  2. WEEKS 03–05

    Feather & Core Layup

    Triple-density cold foam wrapped in compartmentalized goose-feather channels for deep recovery.

  3. WEEKS 06–08

    Velvet Pile Tailoring

    Single-batch velvet panels matched for nap direction, piped along French seams, and hand-fitted.

  4. WEEKS 09–10

    Bench Audit & Dispatch

    Seventy-two-hour load check, batch stamp registration, and white-glove blanket delivery.
